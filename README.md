<h1 align="center">IRIS ITS</h1>
<p align="center"><i>Wani Budal, Wani Mati, IRIS Juara, Harga Mati!</i></p>

**ITS Robot with Intelligent System**, widely known as **IRIS**, represents the [Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS)](https://its.ac.id) in the field of autonomous wheeled soccer robots. Established in 2017, IRIS has specialized in the Middle Size League category and has consistently participated in various prestigious competitions.

Over the years, the team has achieved numerous accolades, showcasing its excellence in robotics and innovation. Comprising talented students from Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember in Surabaya, IRIS embodies the institution’s commitment to advancing technology and fostering future leaders in the field.
